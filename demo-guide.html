Section 1

What is Invexa? (in 30 seconds)

Invexa is an online inventory system for food factories that make fresh, short shelf-life products, such as tofu, plant-based yogurt, soya chaap, pickles and spreads.

It manages the whole journey of a product in one place:

Buy raw materials → Make products → Store in cold rooms → Sell to customers → Handle returns

Because everything is connected, one action automatically updates every other screen. When you sell 24 packs, stock goes down, the invoice is created, the dashboard updates and the audit log records who did it.

⏳

Expiry control

Every pack belongs to a batch with a made-on and expiry date. The system always sends out the oldest-expiry stock first (called FEFO).

🌡️

Cold-chain tracking

Fridge and cold-room temperatures are logged. If one goes out of range, an alert appears straight away.

🔍

Recall in seconds

Pick any batch and see which raw materials went into it and which customers received it.

🏭

Production with recipes

Each product has a recipe. Starting production takes the ingredients out of stock automatically.

🧾

Sales & GST invoices

Orders, dispatch, GST tax invoice, payments, credit limits and returns.

🔐

Secure & role-based

Each person only sees the menus their job needs. Data is encrypted, and every action is recorded.

The demo company is a fictional food manufacturer called "Verdant Soy Foods Pvt. Ltd." with a plant in Pune and cold hubs in Mumbai and Bengaluru. It already has 60 days of realistic history: orders, production, purchases, temperatures and more.
Section 2

Before you start (5 minutes before the meeting)

Do these checks before the audience joins.

  • Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge in a normal window, not Incognito/Private. Make sure the internet is working.
  • Open https://parthagni22.github.io/invexa-inventory-demo/. You should see the Invexa landing page.
  • Click Sign in, then click the Administrator demo account and sign in once. The first time, it takes about a second to load the demo data.
  • Want a clean, fresh dataset? Click Reset demo data in the blue strip at the top of the app, then click Reset data.
  • Sign out: click your name at the top-right, then Sign out. You are back on the landing page, ready to start.
  • Zoom the browser to 90% (press Ctrl + -) if you are on a small screen or sharing on a projector.
All data lives inside your own browser. If you present from a different laptop or browser, it starts with fresh demo data. That's fine, but changes you made earlier won't be there.

Section 5

Screen basics (the same for every login)

Point these out once at the start. They work the same everywhere.

📋

Left menu (sidebar)

Every module. Red numbers next to a menu mean something needs attention, for example orders waiting to be dispatched.

✨

"Generate sample data" buttons

Every screen has a purple dotted button at the top-right (for example ✨ Generate sample orders). One click adds realistic new records, and all linked screens update. This is how you load data in one click.

➕

"+ New" button (top bar)

Create anything from anywhere: Sales Order, Purchase Order, Production Order, Transfer, Adjustment, Item, Customer.

🔔

Bell icon (alerts)

Low stock, expiring batches, temperature problems, late deliveries. Click an alert to jump straight to the right screen.

🔎

Search (or press Ctrl + K)

Type a product, batch number, order number or customer name to find it instantly.

👉

Click any row

Clicking a row in any table opens a side panel with full details and action buttons (Confirm, Dispatch, Print and so on).

Every table has a search box, filters and an Export button that downloads the table as a CSV file (opens in Excel).

Section 7 · Login 2 of 4

🏭 Inventory Manager: the factory story

Goal: show how materials are bought, how products are made, and how stock is kept safe and fresh.

Login: stores@invexa.demo / Store@2026 · After you log in you see: the same Dashboard, but the left menu shows only 12 modules. Sales Orders, Customers, Returns and Settings are hidden.
"Notice the menu is smaller. Sneha, the stores manager, only sees what her job needs."
Part A

Buy raw materials (Purchase Order → Receive goods)

4 min
  1. Left menu, click Purchase Orders.

    Shows every order placed with suppliers and how much has arrived.

  2. Click ⚡ Reorder suggestions.
    A list of materials that are running low, grouped by supplier, with a suggested quantity.
    "The system tells us what to buy and how much."

    If it says "Nothing to reorder", close it and click + New purchase order. Choose any vendor (for example Malwa Agro Commodities), add Soybean – Non-GMO with Qty 500, then click Create PO.

  3. Click Create PO next to any supplier.
    A New Purchase Order form opens, already filled in with the items and prices.

    Keep "Issue to vendor now" ticked and click Create PO.

  4. In the side panel that opens, click Receive goods.

    This is the moment the truck arrives at the factory.

    Leave the quantities as they are. Point out the Vendor lot #, Mfg date and Expiry columns. Change one expiry date if you like.
  5. Click Post goods receipt.
    A message saying "GRN-… posted, batches created". The PO status turns Received.
  6. In the panel, look at Goods receipts and click one of the batch chips (they start with V).
    The batch page shows its expiry date, shelf-life bar and quantity, exactly what you just received.
    "Every bag of raw material now has a lot number and expiry date. That's the start of our traceability."
Part B

Make a product (Production Order)

4 min · the most impressive part
  1. Left menu, click Recipes (BOM), then click the "Premium Tofu – Plain 200 g" card.
    The recipe: soybean, coagulant, pouch and label, with the cost per pack and the profit margin.

    Click Cancel to close it.

  2. Left menu, click Production Orders, then + New production order.
  3. Fill in the form:
    Product: Premium Tofu – Plain 200 g · Quantity: 500
    The form instantly shows the ingredients needed, what is available, the cost per pack and the share of daily plant capacity used.
  4. Click Create order.
    The side panel opens with status Planned and a "Material requirements" table where every line says OK.
  5. Click Issue materials & start.
    Status changes to In Progress. A "Materials issued (FEFO lots)" table shows exactly which soybean lots were used, oldest expiry first.
    "The system automatically takes ingredients out of stock, oldest first, so nothing goes to waste."
  6. Click Record QC & complete.
    Good packs: 495 · Rejected packs: 5 · leave pH, temperature and the rest as they are

    Click Release batch.

    A message saying "Batch TPL2-… released to stock". The status is Completed, with yield and cost per pack.
  7. Click the batch number shown in the panel (next to "Output batch").
    The batch page: shelf-life bar, expiry date, and a Backward trace listing the raw-material lots used.
    "If a customer ever complains, we can see in one click which soybean lot went into this tofu."
You can also just click ✨ Plan sample production to add ready-made production orders, then press Start and Complete on them directly in the table.
Part C

Cold-chain temperature alert

2 min
  1. Left menu, click Warehouses & Cold Chain.
    4 cards (Pune Cold Room, Pune Dry Store, Mumbai Hub, Bengaluru Hub), each with its live temperature, a temperature graph and space used.
  2. On the "Pune Plant · Cold Room A" card, click Log reading.
    Temperature: 6.5 · Note: Door left open

    Click Record.

    An orange message saying "Excursion recorded, alert raised". The card turns red, the bell 🔔 count goes up, and the graph shows a red dot.
    "The cold room should be 0–4°C. The moment it goes above, everyone is alerted."
  3. Log a normal reading to fix it:
    Temperature: 2.5
    "Reading recorded, within range". The card is green again.
Part D

Move stock to a city hub (Transfer)

2 min
  1. Left menu, click Stock Transfers, then + New transfer.
  2. Fill in the form:
    From: Pune Plant · Cold Room A · To: Mumbai Cold Hub · Bhiwandi · Item: Premium Tofu – Plain 200 g · Qty: 48
    The "FEFO picks" column shows which batches will be sent, oldest expiry first.
  3. Click Dispatch transfer.
    The new transfer shows as In Transit (the truck is on the road).
  4. Click the green Receive button on that row.
    Status becomes Completed. The stock is now in Mumbai.
Part E

Wastage and expired stock (optional)

1 min
  1. Left menu, click Batches & Expiry. In the first filter, choose "Expired".
    Expired batches in red.
  2. Click Write off expired, then confirm.
    The expired stock is removed. You can see it under Stock Adjustments with its rupee value.
  3. Sign out (your name at the top-right, then Sign out).

🧾 Sales Executive: the customer story

Part A

Take an order → Dispatch → Invoice

5 min
  1. Left menu, click Customers.

    Click "Pune Fresh Distributors" to show their details: credit limit, how much they owe, the products they buy most, and past orders.

  2. In that panel, click New order.
    The order form opens with the customer already selected, the right warehouse chosen automatically, and their trade discount filled in.
  3. Add products:
    Product: Premium Tofu – Plain 200 g · Qty: 24
    Click + Add item · Product: Soya Chaap 250 g · Qty: 12
    The Available column shows live stock (it turns red if you ask for more than is available). The bottom shows Gross, Discount, CGST + SGST and the Total. A blue box shows the customer's credit limit.
  4. Keep "Confirm order now" ticked and click Create order.
    The order (SO-…) opens with status Confirmed. The "Sales Orders" menu badge goes up by 1.
  5. Click Dispatch.
    A window lists the exact batches picked for each product, earliest expiry first.
    Vehicle: MH12 AB 1234 · Driver: Ramesh

    Click Dispatch & generate invoice.

    "Dispatched, invoice INV-… generated". A "FEFO batch allocation" table appears in the panel.
    "The system picks the oldest stock automatically, so customers always get fresh product and we never waste expiring stock."
  6. Click Tax invoice.
    A professional GST invoice with company GSTIN, FSSAI number, HSN codes, batch numbers and expiry dates, and CGST/SGST.

    You can click Print / Save PDF to show it can be printed. Then close it.

Want to show IGST (another state)? Make the order for "Bengaluru Green Distributors" (Karnataka). The invoice shows IGST instead of CGST + SGST.
Part B

Delivery and payment

2 min
  1. In the same order panel, click Mark delivered.
    Status becomes Delivered.
  2. Click Record payment.
    Amount: type half of the total (for example 1000) · Mode: UPI · Reference: UPI12345

    Click Record payment.

    The payment status shows Partial and the balance due goes down. Record the rest to make it Paid.
Part C

Customer returns some packs

2 min
  1. In the same order panel, click Return.
    The return form opens with your order selected and its batches listed.
  2. Fill in the form:
    Reason: Damaged in transit · Return qty: 2 on the first line · Disposition: Scrap

    Click Post return & credit note.

    "RTN-… posted · credit note CN-…". Click Credit note to show the printable credit note.
    "Good packs can go back into stock (Restock), damaged ones are scrapped and counted as wastage."

1 min

    4 new realistic orders appear instantly, some Confirmed and some Draft, and the dashboard numbers change.

    A message saying "Your role (Sales Executive) doesn't have access to that module", and you are sent back to the Dashboard.

Section 11

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
"Invalid email or password"Click a demo account on the Sign in page instead of typing. Passwords are case-sensitive: Admin@2026, Store@2026, Sales@2026.
"Too many failed attempts"Wait 60 seconds, then try again.
A menu I need is missingYou are logged in as the wrong role. Sign out and log in as Administrator, who sees everything.
"Insufficient stock" when dispatching or transferringLower the quantity (the Available column shows the maximum), or choose a different warehouse, or make more with a Production Order.
"Material shortage" when starting productionLower the quantity, or go to Purchase Orders → Reorder suggestions → Create PO → Receive goods first.
The data looks messy after practisingClick Reset demo data in the blue strip at the top of the app.
Asked to sign in again unexpectedlyThe app locks after 4 hours of no use, or if the tab was closed. Just sign in again; your data is still there.
The page looks blank or stuckPress F5 to refresh. You stay logged in.
The client asks "where is the data stored?""In this demo it's stored encrypted in the browser. In the real product it runs on a secure cloud server with daily backups."
Practise the full flow once the day before. The whole run takes about 30 minutes, and after that it will feel natural.
